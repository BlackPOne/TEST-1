Can I begin if I do not know what I love yet?

Start by noticing the moments that draw you in, then use the questions in each area to reflect. You do not need all the answers before enquiring.

Will this diagram give me all the answers?

The diagram helps you ask questions and see connections. Finding your own direction still calls for exploring your experiences and real-life circumstances.

How is Ikigai different from Insight?

Insight explores your identity, relationships and broader life direction. Ikigai focuses on connecting what you love, your strengths, what the world needs and what you can earn from.

How do I prepare and arrange a session?

Share what you are looking for, then confirm the format, duration, price, location and what is included with THE NAME before arranging a session.
